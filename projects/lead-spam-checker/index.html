What it is

How it works

Two checks, one classification, and one filing

Website form
A form submission starts the workflow, with name, email, query or project brief, and company.
Empty check
If the query field is empty, there is no entry at all.
Brief check
The project brief is validated before it is classified.
AI classification
An AI model reads the enquiry and decides: genuine or spam.
Google Sheets
Genuine and spam queries are filed in separate tabs.
Built in n8n, with the steps running in this order.
1
Order of checks
What I did
Checked the empty query field first.
Why it mattered
So an empty submission never creates a junk row.
2
Brief validation
What I did
Validated the project brief before classifying.
Why it mattered
So only enquiries with real content reach the classifier.
3
The classifier
What I did
Used an AI model instead of fixed keyword rules.
Why it mattered
So it can judge what an enquiry says, rather than only match words.
4
Spam handling
What I did
Kept spam in its own tab instead of deleting it.
Why it mattered
So nothing is lost and spam can still be reviewed.
5
Categories
What I did
Kept it to two categories: genuine or spam.
Why it mattered
It keeps the decision simple. A borderline enquiry has to land in one of the two, so a third category is a next step.
